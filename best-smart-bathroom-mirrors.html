  • Smart means lighting and power at this price. Between $79.99 and $299.99, smart bathroom mirrors offer LED lighting with onboard controls and, on the Mepplzian, power sockets and USB ports. Voice control and built-in displays start above this range.
  • Decide on storage first. Five of our seven picks are medicine cabinets with shelving behind the glass; the Habison and ANYHI are flat wall mirrors. Cabinets cost more and sit deeper off the wall.
  • Measure before you buy. Sizes here run from 16x24 inches (the KAASUNES) to a 31.5-inch square (the WisHomee black cabinet). A mirror should sit a few inches narrower than your vanity.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do smart bathroom mirrors need Wi-Fi or a touchscreen?

No. In the under-$300 range, smart means built-in LED lighting with onboard controls, plus extras like power sockets and USB ports on some models (the Mepplzian in our guide has both). Mirrors with touchscreens and voice assistants exist, but they cost several times more and put delicate electronics in a humid room.

Can I install a smart mirror or LED medicine cabinet myself?

Flat LED mirrors like the Habison and ANYHI hang much like a standard mirror, so most people can manage the job in an afternoon. Medicine cabinets are heavier, and models with built-in outlets like the Mepplzian call for proper in-wall wiring. Check the wall you plan to use before you buy, and budget for an electrician if it lacks power.

Which smart bathroom mirror should I buy?

Among the best smart bathroom mirrors we compared, the KAASUNES LED Medicine Cabinet 16x24inch is the one we recommend first. At $194.00 it combines face-height LED lighting, hidden storage, and a tempered glass and aluminum build in a size that fits most single vanities. Go bigger with the WisHomee if you have a double vanity, or save with the $79.99 ANYHI if you need the lighting alone.
