What We Liked

The clearest advantage of the GarveeHome 60x36 LED bathroom mirror is scale. A single continuous mirror across a 60-inch vanity looks more intentional than two separate mirrors hung with a gap between them, and it avoids the clutter of multiple light fixtures competing for wall space. The aluminum frame also matters more than it might seem: at this width, a thinly backed mirror can bow slightly over time, while the frame keeps the glass flat against the wall. Built-in LED lighting is the other practical win, since it replaces the vanity sconces most double-sink setups need and simplifies both the install and the electrical work behind the wall. For a remodel that already has an electrician on site, folding the mirror's light source into the mirror itself instead of running two separate sconce circuits can also trim a line item off the overall project cost.

What Could Be Better

At $359.99, the GarveeHome 60x36 LED bathroom mirror costs noticeably more than the WisHomee and Janboe medicine cabinets in this guide, and none of that premium buys you storage. If your bathroom is short on counter space, a medicine cabinet that doubles as a mirror will do more work for less money. Size cuts both ways too: a 60-inch mirror needs a wall wide enough to clear light switches, towel bars, and door swings, so measure before you buy rather than assuming it will fit because your vanity is also wide. Installation is also a two-person job in most cases, given the weight and width of tempered glass this size. The listing does not specify a dimmer or adjustable color temperature either, so if you want warmer light for evening routines and brighter, cooler light for makeup or shaving, confirm that feature before buying rather than assuming a mirror this price includes it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the GarveeHome 60x36 LED bathroom mirror too big for a standard vanity?

It depends on your vanity width. At 60 inches long, this mirror is sized for double-sink vanities that run roughly five feet or wider. On a single-sink vanity under 48 inches wide, the mirror will overhang the counter on both sides. Measure your vanity top before ordering rather than relying on the sink count alone.

Does the GarveeHome mirror come with storage?

No. It is a mirror only, framed in aluminum with a tempered glass panel. The WisHomee and Janboe medicine cabinets covered in this guide add storage behind the mirror door, which is the main trade-off against the GarveeHome's larger, uninterrupted glass surface. If you need both a large mirror and storage, plan on adding a separate cabinet elsewhere in the bathroom rather than expecting one fixture to do both jobs.

Is the GarveeHome worth $359.99 compared to a cheaper medicine cabinet?

That depends on whether you need mirror size or storage more. We compared the GarveeHome against the Janboe LED Medicine Cabinet at $239.99 and found the price difference buys roughly 50% more mirror surface and no seam across a double vanity. If storage matters more than size, the cheaper Janboe or WisHomee cabinets do more for less money.

Final Verdict

Our verdict in this GarveeHome 60x36 LED bathroom review: the mirror earns its price if you have a double-sink vanity wide enough to carry it and no need for extra storage behind the glass.
