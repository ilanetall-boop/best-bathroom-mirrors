Things to Know Before You Buy

  • Measure your vanity width before you buy. A mirror wider than the counter crowds sconces and looks off balance.
  • Plug-in LED mirrors mount like a standard mirror and run off a nearby outlet. Hardwired models need a junction box and usually a licensed electrician.
  • Anti-fog demister pads need a live connection to work, so plan your outlet or wiring location around where the mirror hangs.
  • Mirrors over about 30 pounds go up more safely with two people. All seven mirrors here ship with wall-mounting brackets, but the largest ones still deserve a helper.
  • Locate at least two studs before you drill, or budget for drywall anchors rated for the mirror's weight.

Frequently Asked Questions

How difficult is bathroom mirror installation for a plug-in LED mirror?

Most plug-in LED mirrors, including the ones in this comparison, mount with wall brackets similar to hanging a heavy picture frame. You still need to locate studs or use properly rated drywall anchors, and mirrors over about 30 pounds go up more safely with two people. Because these are plug-in rather than hardwired, you are not opening electrical boxes or running new wiring.

Do I need an electrician to install an LED bathroom mirror?

Not for the plug-in models covered here. They connect to a standard outlet the same way a lamp would. Hardwired LED mirrors that wire directly into a junction box are a different category and generally do require a licensed electrician, which is why we left those out of this comparison.

What size mirror fits a double vanity?

For a standard double vanity, look at mirrors in the 50 to 60-inch width range, like the GarveeHome 60x36 or Delma 30x50 in this comparison, rather than two smaller mirrors side by side. A single continuous mirror avoids alignment gaps and keeps LED lighting consistent across both sink areas.
