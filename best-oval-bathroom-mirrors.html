Things to Know Before You Buy

  • Oval bathroom mirrors trade square corners for a softer curve, not more reflective surface. An oval mirror listed at the same length and width as a rectangular one shows less usable reflection, since the corners are cut away. Measure your vanity width against the mirror's widest point, not its diagonal.
  • Several Janboe oval medicine cabinets on this list share the same footprint.   • Brushed nickel and matte black dominate the oval mirror hardware in this category. If your faucet and cabinet pulls are already brushed nickel, matching that finish on the mirror frame keeps the vanity from looking mismatched.
  • Medicine-cabinet versions cost more than plain wall mirrors of a similar size. Frequently Asked Questions

What is the standard size for an oval bathroom mirror?

Most oval bathroom mirrors sized for a single vanity run between 20 by 16 inches and 27 by 22 inches, similar to the JJUUYOU and Moen mirrors in this guide. For a double vanity or a wider single-sink counter, look at the 20 by 32 inch Janboe medicine cabinets instead, since a single oval mirror much wider than 28 inches is uncommon in this shape.

Do oval medicine cabinets lose storage space compared with rectangular ones?

Yes, slightly. The curved corners on an oval medicine cabinet cut into the usable shelf width near the top and bottom of the interior, even when the door opening is the same height as a rectangular model. If maximizing storage matters more than the shape, a rectangular medicine cabinet of the same width will hold more.

Can I install an oval bathroom mirror in a rental without drilling into the wall?

It depends on the weight. The JJUUYOU at 20 by 16 inches is light enough for heavy-duty adhesive strips rated for the mirror's weight, but the larger Moen and the Janboe medicine cabinets are heavier and should go into wall studs or use the manufacturer's mounting bracket rather than adhesive alone.
