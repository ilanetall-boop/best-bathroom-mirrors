Frequently Asked Questions

Should I buy a mirror cabinet or a separate mirror and cabinet?

Pick by storage need and wall depth. A mirror cabinet like the Tangkula ($69.99) puts shelving behind the glass but adds 6.5 inches of depth to the wall. A large mirror above a vanity cabinet, like the InfiniGlass, gives you more glass and light but no hidden storage. Bathrooms with pedestal sinks lean toward a cabinet; vanity bathrooms usually have storage below already.

Can I install a recessed mirror cabinet myself?

Only if you are comfortable opening drywall. A recessed model like the TokeShimi requires cutting an opening between two studs, checking the bay for pipes and wiring, and framing it square. A surface-mount cabinet like the Tangkula screws to the wall in under an hour with no cutting.
