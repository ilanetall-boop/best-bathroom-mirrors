Frequently Asked Questions

What is a shaver socket on a bathroom mirror?

Shaver socket is the British term for a power point built into a bathroom fitting. On the mirrors in this guide it means outlets or USB ports wired into the mirror or cabinet, so a razor, trimmer, or toothbrush charges at face level. Our top pick, the Mepplzian, builds both sockets and USB charging into its cabinet.

Can I install a mirror with a shaver socket myself?

You can mount the cabinet yourself with a stud finder, a level, and a second person to hold the weight. The wiring depends on the model: plug-in units go straight into an existing outlet, while hardwired units need a licensed electrician. Check the manual for the wiring type before you buy, not after the box arrives.

Is it safe to have outlets in a bathroom mirror cabinet?

Yes, provided the circuit has GFCI protection, which US electrical code requires for bathroom receptacles. Buy a unit with an ETL or UL safety listing, keep cords clear of the sink basin, and unplug heated styling tools when you finish. Cabinet sockets also sit behind the mirror door on most models, which keeps splash exposure low.
