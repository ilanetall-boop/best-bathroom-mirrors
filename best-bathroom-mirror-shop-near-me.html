Frequently Asked Questions

Is it better to buy a bathroom mirror online or find a bathroom mirror shop near me?

For most shoppers, buying online wins on selection and price. The seven mirrors compared in this guide range from $56.92 to $186.29 and include verified buyer ratings you can check before ordering, while a local shop typically carries a handful of styles at a retail markup. The one advantage a local shop keeps is same-day pickup if you need a replacement mirror immediately.

What size bathroom mirror fits my vanity?

Measure your vanity width first. A single sink usually pairs with a mirror in the 24 to 32 inch range, like the Sweetcrispy or DUMOS in this comparison. A wide single vanity fits a 36 to 50 inch mirror like the MYSUNORIA or Delma, and a true double-sink vanity needs a 60 inch span like the GarveeHome.

Do LED bathroom mirrors need to be hardwired?

No. The LED mirrors in this comparison plug into a standard wall outlet near the vanity, the same way a nightlight or hair dryer would. If you want a cleaner look with no visible cord, an electrician can wire the mirror directly into a switched circuit, but that's an optional upgrade, not a requirement.
