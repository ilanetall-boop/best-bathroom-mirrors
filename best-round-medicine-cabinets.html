Things to Know Before You Buy

  • Genuine round medicine cabinets with interior shelving are rare. Most "round" bathroom picks on the market are mirrors without a cabinet body, so check the product description before buying if storage matters to you.
  • Tempered glass is worth paying for. It resists cracking in shipping and won't cloud the way cheaper acrylic mirrors do after a few years of bathroom humidity.
  • Measure your wall space before ordering — round mirrors in the 24- to 28-inch range need more clearance than a same-width rectangular mirror because of the curve.
  • LED-lit options add roughly $30 to $90 to the price but can replace a separate vanity light, which offsets some of the cost.
  • Check what mounting hardware is included — some listings ship with plastic anchors that aren't rated for glass this heavy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are there any true round medicine cabinets with storage available?

Round medicine cabinets with interior shelving are rare, since a circular cabinet door with a working hinge is harder and more expensive to manufacture than a rectangular one. If storage is a priority, pair one of the mirrors above with a separate cabinet or shelf rather than searching for a round cabinet.

What size round mirror should I buy for my vanity?

Measure the width of your vanity or sink area and leave at least 2 inches of clearance on each side. Most single-sink vanities fit a 24- to 28-inch round mirror comfortably; for double vanities, use two mirrors sized to each sink or one wide frameless option like our top pick.

Is a mirror with built-in LED lighting worth the extra cost?

It depends on your existing lighting. If your vanity light bar is already bright, a plain mirror paired with good bulbs works fine and costs less. If your bathroom lighting is dim or outdated, an LED mirror like the Sweetcrispy pick solves both problems at once without adding a second fixture.
