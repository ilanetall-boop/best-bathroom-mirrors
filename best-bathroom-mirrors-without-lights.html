  • Every mirror in this guide includes LED lighting, but the light is optional. A touch or physical switch lets you leave it off and use the mirror as a standard bathroom mirror without lights.

  • Frame material stays consistent across this lineup: all seven use tempered glass mounted in an aluminum frame, so weight and mounting hardware needs are similar regardless of price.
  • Size ranges from a compact 24x32 mirror up to a 60x36 double-vanity model, so measure your wall space and check outlet or wiring access before you choose.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are LED bathroom mirrors actually mirrors without lights?

They can be. Every mirror in this guide has a physical or touch switch, so you can leave the LED off and use it as a plain bathroom mirror without lights whenever you don't need the extra brightness. The light is optional rather than fixed on.

Do I need an electrician to install a switchable LED mirror?

It depends on the model. Some switchable LED mirrors run on a battery or plug into a standard outlet near the vanity, while others are designed for hardwiring behind the wall. Check the specific product listing for wiring requirements before you buy, and budget for an electrician if hardwiring is required.

What size bathroom mirror should I buy for a single vanity?

For a single sink, a mirror close to the width of your vanity counter, generally 24 to 36 inches wide, covers the space without looking undersized. The 24x32 and 28x36 options in this guide fit that range, while the 60-inch GarveeHome is better reserved for double-sink vanities.
